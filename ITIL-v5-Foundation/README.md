# ITIL® (Version 5) Foundation Certification

Welcome to the **ITIL® (Version 5) Foundation** course repository. This guide provides an overview of the latest evolution in IT Service Management (ITSM), designed for the modern, AI-driven digital landscape.

## 🚀 Course Overview

ITIL (Version 5), announced in January 2026, is the global standard for managing digital products and services. While it preserves the robust foundation of ITIL 4, it introduces a strategic shift toward **AI-native governance**, **Experience design**, and **End-to-end product lifecycles**.

### Key Learning Objectives

-   **Modern Concepts:** Understand the evolution from traditional IT service management to Digital Product & Service Management.

-   **Value Co-Creation:** Master the art of structured service relationships and value co-creation models.

-   **AI Integration:** Learn how ITIL supports ethical and responsible AI implementation through the new AI Governance extension.

-   **Value Streams:** Explore Value Stream Mapping and Management within the updated Value System.


## 📅 Course Syllabus

The course is structured into foundational modules that transition learners from basic terminology to complex service value systems.

1.  **Key ITIL Terms and Definitions:** Product, Service, Value, and Experience.

2.  **Service Relationships:** Exploring the dynamics between providers, consumers, and stakeholders.

3.  **The ITIL Value System:** Governance, Guiding Principles, and the Value Chain.

4.  **The Product Lifecycle:** Integrating DevOps, Agile, and Lean best practices.

5.  **Continual Improvement:** Applying the 7 Guiding Principles in a digital-first world.

6.  **The Four Dimensions:** Organizations & People, Information & Technology, Partners & Suppliers, and Value Streams & Processes.


## 🏆 Certification Path

ITIL (Version 5) streamlines the journey to **ITIL Master** through three primary streams.

### The Learning Streams

-   **ITIL® Practice Manager:** Focused on operational excellence and practice-based capabilities.

-   **ITIL® Managing Professional:** Focused on running successful IT-enabled products and services.

-   **ITIL® Strategic Leader:** Focused on the alignment of IT strategy with business goals.


### Transition for ITIL 4 Professionals

If you are already ITIL 4 certified, you don't start from scratch:

-   **Managing Professional Transition (v5):** A dedicated module to bridge the gap between ITIL 4 and Version 5.

-   **Core Modules:** The _ITIL Transformation_ module is a universal requirement across multiple paths, emphasizing sustainable change.
